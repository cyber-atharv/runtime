The Problem & Our Solution

Making Walking Easier and Pain-Free

Traditional knee braces are often stiff, bulky, and lock your leg in place. FlexAssist gently assists your natural knee motion with lightweight support springs when you need it most.

TODAY'S PROBLEMS

What Patients Struggle With

  • Weak Knees & Aging: Climbing stairs, standing up, or walking long distances feels exhausting due to knee pain or reduced leg strength.
  • Heavy & Stiff Braces: Old-fashioned braces lock the knee tightly, forcing people to walk in an awkward, stiff-legged way.
  • Painful Joint Wear: Without mechanical assistance, knee joints absorb heavy shocks with every step, worsening arthritis and joint wear.
  • Hard to Customize: Standard medical braces cannot easily be adjusted as a patient regains strength during recovery.
THE FLEXASSIST SOLUTION

How FlexAssist Helps You

  • Smooth Natural Motion: The smart hinge aligns with your knee to give extra lifting power during every step and deep bend.
  • Light & Tough Metal: Key parts are precision-cut from aircraft-grade aluminum, hollowed out to stay light while carrying full body weight.
  • Adjustable Support Springs: Easily tune or swap the built-in spring cartridges to provide anywhere from gentle to strong walking support.
  • Built-in Safety Stops: Physical safety limiters protect your knee from bending backwards or over-stretching during sudden movements.

Why We Chose 3-Axis CNC Milling

BEST CHOICE

3-Axis CNC Milling

Why It Works: Perfect for flat, shaped metal plates with screw holes and weight-saving hollow pockets. The entire part is precision-milled from solid aluminum in one secure setup.

  • Pinpoint exact sizing (within 0.015 mm)
  • Extreme strength to carry full body weight
  • Hollowing out pockets saves weight
NOT SUITABLE

Lathe Turning

Why It Fails: Lathes can only spin round, cylindrical rods like shafts. They cannot carve flat brackets, side mounting holes, or hollow pockets.

  • Only works for round pipes & rods
  • Cannot make flat side brackets
NOT STRONG ENOUGH

Plastic 3D Printing

Why It Fails: 3D printed plastic is built in layers that can split or crack under sudden shock when a patient walks down stairs.

  • Plastic layers can snap under load
  • Unsafe for carrying human weight
